Knuu Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

About Knuu

Knuu helps you discover sourced trivia and learn about nearby places on iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains how information is used by the app, its optional community features and this policy website. Contact knuu.support@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Location and nearby places

With your permission, Knuu uses your location to center the map, calculate nearby distances, identify your city and country, and find relevant place stories. Background discovery uses background location only when you enable it and grant the required iOS permission. Your location or search area may be sent to Apple for maps and location lookup, and to Wikimedia services for geographically relevant articles and images. Integrated Google mapping or place services may process search, location, network and device information when those services are used. Knuu does not publish your live location or a personal travel trail to other users.

Information stored on your devices

Knuu stores preferences, saved place identifiers, recent discoveries, the daily spoken-trivia allowance and replay history, cached place stories, downloaded resources, generated voice audio and a last-known location/city/country on your device. These records support offline use and avoid repeated narration. If you add private named places or address pins, their names and coordinates are also saved. Device backups may include app data under your Apple settings. Optional Pro discovery interests are stored locally, separately for each traveler account and guest profile. They prioritize nearby topics without being sent as RevenueCat customer attributes or synced to your cloud collection. You can reset these choices in Discovery interests; deleting a traveler account clears its interests on that device.

iCloud and Apple Watch

Guest saved stories may sync through your Apple account when iCloud is available. In versions with traveler accounts, signed-in Knuu saved stories and collected/known trivia use private Supabase account sync instead of the guest iCloud library. Private named pins remain in local app storage and may be included in device backups. Apple manages iCloud and backups. The companion Watch app receives a small snapshot of nearby place stories and your latest iPhone discovery through WatchConnectivity. The Watch can use its own location when you request nearby stories. These features do not publish your information to the community.

Optional community contributions

You can read published community stories without becoming a contributor. If you accept contributor access, Knuu processes a display name, member identifier, role and a secure access token. Submitted story text, source links, place names and place coordinates, review feedback and moderation records are stored using Supabase. Approved stories and contributor display names can be visible to other users. Reports include the reported story, your reason and a device-generated reporter identifier used to limit abuse. Do not include private addresses or personal information in a public contribution without the appropriate permission.

Payments

Knuu is free to download, with five new trivia discoveries per day. A new reveal or first playback uses one shared slot; reopening and replays do not spend another. Optional Knuu Pro monthly, yearly and one-time lifetime purchases, when available, increase the daily allowance to ten and include themed story collections and personal discovery interests. The themed collections organize existing sourced stories; those individual stories remain discoverable on the free plan. Existing account collection sync stays available on the free plan. Apple handles live App Store billing; Knuu does not receive payment-card details. RevenueCat processes an anonymous installation ID or your Knuu account ID, purchase history and access status, along with paywall and Customer Center interactions, to provide purchases, restores, support and purchase analytics. Local Debug builds may use RevenueCat Test Store for simulated purchases without real charges. Live payments remain disabled until configured. Builds without enabled live or test configuration do not initialize RevenueCat. Daily discovery usage stays on the device.

Voice, notifications and tracking

Breeze narration is generated or played locally using the bundled offline voice engine and audio. Knuu does not send narration text to a speech-generation API. Notification permissions support discovery alerts; Knuu does not require notifications for reading stories. Knuu does not add advertising trackers, sell your personal information or share your location for advertising. Apple, mapping providers, Wikimedia, Supabase and the hosting service may process network requests, IP addresses or operational diagnostics under their own policies. Knuu has not added a separate behavioral analytics SDK.

Retention, deletion and your choices

You can change location and notification permissions in iOS Settings, turn off discovery, remove saved places and private pins, and clear recent passes using the available app controls. Removing the app removes its local app storage, while iCloud records, backups and server-side community records may remain separately. Contributor account deletion is available in Community settings: it removes contributor access and unpublished suggestions; approved sourced stories remain without your contributor name. Moderation and security records may be retained where needed to operate the service or meet legal obligations. Contact us for help with access, correction or deletion requests. We do not promise that deleting a public contribution removes copies already saved or shared by others. In versions with traveler accounts, deleting your traveler account removes its login, online profile, saved stories and collected/known records, plus its collection cache on the iPhone where you delete it. Guest/iCloud records, separate contributor access, backups and caches on other devices are handled separately. Authentication and security logs may be retained under the service provider’s policies.

Service providers and international processing

Knuu uses Apple services for maps, iCloud, Watch communication and App Store purchases; Wikimedia for public articles and images; and Supabase for community functionality and, in versions offering traveler accounts, authentication and private collection sync. Google SDKs are integrated for supported mapping/place features. Community infrastructure is configured in Singapore, and service providers may process data in other countries. Their own privacy policies apply to their services. The public privacy and support website is hosted on GitHub Pages and has no Knuu analytics, advertising or data-entry forms. GitHub may process web request information under its privacy policy.

Children and changes to this policy

Knuu is a general-audience travel and trivia app. Community contributors should not post personal information about children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact knuu.support@gmail.com. We may update this policy as features or data practices change and will update its effective date. Material changes will be communicated through the app or this page as appropriate.

Contacting support

If you email knuu.support@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send so we can answer your request. The support mailbox is hosted by Google Gmail, which processes these messages under its privacy policy. We use support messages to respond to you and handle support or privacy requests. Contact us to request deletion of a support message; records may be retained where needed to resolve an ongoing request or meet legal obligations.

Traveler accounts and private trivia collections

In app versions that offer traveler accounts, you may create an optional email/password account. Supabase Auth processes your email address and password to authenticate you; Knuu stores session credentials in the device Keychain rather than saving your password. Your private account profile includes your chosen display name, avatar and color. Your saved story identifiers, collected and known marks, and sourced story snapshots sync to Supabase under your account identifier. A snapshot can include the place name, place coordinates, question, fact, background and source attribution; it is not a log of your device location. Knuu caches this collection and pending edits on your device for offline use. Signing out switches to the separate guest collection; guest data is copied into an account only when you choose to import it. Collections are not published to the community. Email confirmation and password-recovery codes are sent by Supabase Auth through Google Gmail SMTP.

KNUU SUPPORT

How can we help?

Email knuu.support@gmail.com for help with Knuu, account access, collections, privacy requests or a story correction.

Account and collection help

Open Profile, then Sign in or create an account. You can customize your profile, reset your password using an emailed code, and delete your account in the account settings. Guest collections stay separate unless you choose to import them.

Nearby discovery

Live place discovery requires internet access and location permission. Select a nearby place to look up a sourced story. Not every place has a reliable matching article.

Audio help

Open Voice & audio to test narration and adjust its boost. When connected to a car, adjust the vehicle’s voice-channel volume while the test plays, with the vehicle parked.

Report a story issue

Include the place name and source link, the issue you found, and your app version. Please avoid sending passwords, verification codes or precise private addresses.

Privacy and deletion requests

See our privacy policy. Email the support address if you need help with access, correction or deletion.
